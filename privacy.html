Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Planner is a desktop app for Windows, made by a-mend (“we”). This policy explains what data Planner uses and what happens to it. The short version: your data stays on your computer and with Google. We never receive it.

Data stored on your computer

The tasks and events you add are saved in a file on your own computer (in %APPDATA%\Planner). Planner has no accounts and no servers, and doesn't collect analytics, crash reports or usage data. Nothing you type into Planner is sent to us.

Google Calendar data

Google Calendar sync is optional and off until you choose Settings → Connect Google Calendar. If you connect, Planner asks Google for permission to view and edit events on your calendars (the calendar.events permission). Planner only uses your primary calendar.

What Planner accesses. Events on your primary calendar from 30 days ago through one year ahead. Google sends these events to your computer. From each event, Planner keeps only its title, date, start and end time, and Google's ID for the event, and ignores everything else (such as descriptions, locations and guests).

How Planner uses it. Only to provide two-way sync that you can see in the app:

  • to show your Google Calendar events in Planner's calendar, and
  • to add, update or delete events in your Google Calendar when you add, change or delete those events in Planner.

Planner never changes or deletes Google Calendar events except as a result of what you do in Planner.

Where it's stored. The event details above, and the sign-in token Google issues to Planner, are stored only on your computer, in %APPDATA%\Planner. Data travels directly between your computer and Google over an encrypted connection.

Sharing. We don't receive, store, sell, rent or share your Google data with anyone. It isn't used for advertising, and it isn't used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting your data

  • Stop syncing: in Planner, open Settings → Disconnect. This deletes Planner's Google sign-in token from your computer.
  • Revoke access in Google: visit myaccount.google.com/connections, select Planner and remove its access.
  • Delete everything: delete the %APPDATA%\Planner folder and Planner.exe. Disconnecting or deleting Planner never deletes events from your Google Calendar.

Children

Planner isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
